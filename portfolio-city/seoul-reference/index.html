10 항공뷰

Olympic Bridge 올림픽대교

This is a relatively newer vehicular bridge across the Han River, located in the eastern part of the city. The Han River, about a mile wide, divides Seoul across its entire east-west width. The development of the southern banks from the 1960s on meant that there was a huge need to construct a few dozen new bridges. Most of them were thrown up rather hastily and have very utilitarian architecture, and some were built so shoddily that one collapsed and one had to be rebuilt. The Olympic Bridge is mercifully an exception, with its distinctive architecture. However, an Army helicopter hired for the installation of one architectural element crashed at the construction site, killing all soldiers on board.
